Privacy policy

AI Lab Tycoon collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, and it never connects to the internet.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you or how you play. It contains no third-party code that does, such as advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

What stays on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves on your device: the endings you have found, your settings (sound, music volume), a run in progress, and a counter of runs played. These stay on your device. They are included in your device's own backups (iCloud or a computer), like other app data, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Network and permissions

The app has no network access: everything it shows is inside the app. It asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.
