Your progress

The game saves on your device: the endings you have found, your settings, and a run in progress. Nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your progress; your device's own backups include it, like other app data.

Starting over

When a run ends, choose Play again on the ending screen to start a fresh run. The endings you found stay in Game › Endings found.

Devices

  • iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.
  • The game plays in landscape on iPhone. On iPad it works in any orientation.
  • On iPhone Duo the game fills the inner display and keeps running when you fold or unfold. If you close the phone during a run, the outer display shows your cash, era and training progress; tap Keep playing to carry on there.

Sound
