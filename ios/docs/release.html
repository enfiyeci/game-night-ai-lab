    Single-player management game: you run an AI lab through five eras, balancing capability, safety, money and a board.     What I checked against Apple, today

    • In the iOS 27.1 SDK, the only way to draw on the Duo's outer display while it is open is CameraCaptureAccessory, and Apple's article says the system shows it "only when the device is open, your app is in the foreground, and a capture session is running." ExternalNonInteractiveAccessory is for external displays and AirPlay. So a glance on the outer display of an open Duo would mean running the camera, which this game has no reason to do. The glance shows when the Duo is closed instead. Read in full: Apple's CameraCaptureAccessory, ExternalNonInteractiveAccessory and sceneAccessory(content:) pages, "Registering a camera capture accessory on iPhone Duo", the Duo HIG, and "Preparing your app for iPhone Duo".
    • Hinge: onHingeChange with DeviceHinge status closed, partially open or fully open (SwiftUI, iOS 27.1). The crease: GeometryProxy.reservedRegions(kind: .division), active only while half-folded. Both confirmed in the SDK's interface files; the simulator reported "open" at launch.
    • Filling the inner display needs the iOS 27.1 SDK; the iOS 27 SDK leaves room by the status bar, and the iOS 26 SDK shows the app in the middle with empty space (Apple's "Three steps" page). Xcode 27.1 needs macOS Tahoe 26.6 or later; this Mac has it.
    • Xcode 27.1 lists a simulator bug: an app using CameraCaptureAccessory can use both displays without a camera. A simulator test of that route would have looked like it worked.
    • From the duo-party-games research (read in full, not re-checked today): no App Store setting limits an app to the Duo; "iPhone" and "Duo" stay out of the app name; Guideline 2.5.19 bans hidden or dormant features, so describe the Duo behaviour in the review notes.
