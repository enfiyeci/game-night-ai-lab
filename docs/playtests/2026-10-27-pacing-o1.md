## Crowding

"Game-opened" leaves out a summit the player opened from the menu (a bot does that on era 5's first day; the game did
not interrupt for it). Target: at most 2 clock-stopping prompts in any 30 s.

| Bot | Same-day stacks per game | Most in any 30 s, game-opened (median / p90 / max) | Most in any 30 s, all |
|---|---|---|---|
| careful, before | 3.9 | 3 / 4 / 4 | 4 / 4 / 4 |
| careful, after | 1.1 | 3 / 3 / 4 | 4 / 4 / 5 |
| balanced, before | 2.6 | 3 / 4 / 4 | 4 / 5 / 5 |
| balanced, after | 0.8 | 3 / 3 / 3 | 4 / 4 / 4 |

- The same-day stacks that remain are the summit a bot opens on era 5's first day (every careful game, 30 of 40
  balanced) and four cards in 80 games whose deadlines left no later day.
- The era change no longer shares a day with a cash pause, the President no longer shares a day with the training
  hazard, and cards no longer land beside set pieces.
- **What still breaks the 2-in-30-s target:** the President's second call (era 4, a week before the end), the era 4
  board meeting six days later and the era change the day after. That is three fixed set pieces inside 21 s, in 33 of
  40 careful and 30 of 40 balanced games. The director does not move fixed days; moving the President's day is an
  owner decision (see the plan).
- Six balanced games show the era change and a cash pause inside 30 s: the bot's own spending at the round start
  makes cash urgent after the era recap is on screen, so the pause comes on a later day instead of folding into the
  recap.

## Quiet

Longest stretch with no decision and no payoff did not change (careful 77.1 / 90 / 90 s, balanced 64.3 / 77.1 /
77.1 s for the whole game). The bots act at every round mark, so the quiet backstop (pull the next scenario card
forward after 90 s) never fires for them; it is covered by a unit test. Cards per era are unchanged (careful 6.6, 6.4,
4.6, 4.5, 4.6; balanced moved by 0.1 in eras 4 and 5).

One behaviour change rides on this: a President's call the director holds past its round's first day (20 of 75 calls
in the reviewer's 40 bot games, all by one day) lapses a round later, as any call opened mid-round does. The bots take
his call only at a round start, so they now meet him a round later in those games.
