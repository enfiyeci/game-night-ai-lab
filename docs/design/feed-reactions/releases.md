# Model releases and rival releases

Posts for launch day, the few weeks after, and every rival release. `{model}` is the player's newest
model and `{lab}` the player's lab. Handles are the personas in `docs/design/feed-personas.md`.

Launch templates are picked by what the model is, following `sim/data/launch.js` and
`sim/feed.js`: its flags, channel, press average, price, thinking effort, capacity, version jump
and rank. A launch shows up to five posts; follow-up reception shows one or two a turn for three turns.
