# Finance planning research (2026-09-25)

Research behind the finance planner screen (compute goals by era against monthly costs and cash).
Mockups: `docs/design/mockups/K2-finance-plan.html` (directions `#a`, `#b`, `#c`), built on
`finance-plan.js` and the seed-4 history in `finance-seed4.json`.

## Reading order

1. `games-finance-screens-2026-09-25.md`: finance, budget and projection screens in 14 management
   games, with 16 screenshots in `shots/` (`01-` to `14-`). Headline: none of them offers an
   interactive forward projection; the genre shows history and fixed limits.
2. `real-labs-compute-plans-2026-09-25.md`: how OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Meta and the hyperscalers
   state compute goals (gigawatts by a year, then dollars), unit costs per gigawatt, lead times,
   and compute spend against revenue. Epoch AI chart captures are `shots/50-epoch-*.jpg`.
