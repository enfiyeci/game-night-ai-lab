Where the endings come from

Today no ending reads the constitution, and the one takeover ending it could shape is so rare that the bots never reached it in 2,600 runs. The research gives you a map of twelve sourced futures keyed to the game's own hard lines, fifteen techniques for making an ending visibly earned, and ten questions the long session has to answer.

This page prepares that session. It shows options and evidence; it picks no endings. You can mark reactions as you read (optional) and copy them with the bar at the bottom.

01

What this means for the redesign

Each finding has its evidence under it. The first three come from the game's code and bot runs; the rest from the research.

  1. No ending reads the constitution today, and the "written versus learned" drift is never used.The ending checks, the finale and the film files never look at hard lines or rulings. learnedConstitution (sim/constitution.js:215-225) is called nowhere, and it still rolls dice, against your 2026-09-26 no-dice decision. Your first want needs new wiring, not just new films.
  2. The quiet takeover is reachable but rare; catastrophic misalignment ended 37% of the bot runs.Zero of 2,600 bot runs, and none in the reachability probe's default 100 seeds per policy. Its one recorded witness is the probe's unchecked policy at seed 163 (tests/fixtures/ending-witnesses.json, replayed here). If constitution-shaped futures hang only on the quiet takeover, few players will see them.
  3. The constitution's reach is small: its strongest effect on any ending is one extra escalation stage before a quiet takeover.The other seven lines nudge misuse, revenue, users, staff trust or event cards. Of the four values set by the six rulings, only candor is read anywhere (Lumen turns flattering below 0.4); caution, deference and userFirst are read nowhere.
  4. The eight hard lines split into lines that decide who ends up holding power and lines that decide how that ruler treats people.Power: accept-shutdown, no-power-grab, no-deceive-lab, no-autonomy-grab. Treatment: honest, privacy, no-manipulation. Deference (how far the AI obeys its lab) is the main hinge. Both futures agents reached this from different sources; both label the mapping as their inference.
  5. Every constitution keeps exactly three of eight lines, so a missing line is the normal case.Each line is missing from 35 of the 56 possible three-line sets. A futures system that reads "line absent" as "line broken" would push almost every run dark. The rulings' values also move in narrow ranges: deference only 0.25 to 0.83, caution 0.38 to 0.65 (checked in this session).
  6. Endings feel earned when each outcome traces to one or two named investments; they feel arbitrary when many choices collapse into one number.Mass Effect 2: each unbought ship upgrade kills a specific crew member on screen. Mass Effect 3 folded a campaign into one score, drew a backlash, and shipped a free epilogue that reads earlier choices.
  7. The best games show which way you are heading but hide the cutoff, and warn through characters, music and the world, not cards.Undertale's emptying towns and save-point text; Frostpunk's music change after its moral line; Suzerain's retire request. This fits your no-extra-warning-card rule better than it first looks.
  8. The end screen is where games prove causation, and ours proves little.It names no deciding decision, and the finale tells the player its choices "do not change the ending you earned" (ui/screens/end.js). This War of Mine gives each survivor an epilogue chosen from a tally of what the player did.
  9. Futures after the transition differ less in comfort than in who holds the controls and whether the AI is honest with them.AI 2027's race and slowdown endings both have cures, basic income and robot factories; they differ in who the AI answers to. Films have to show power and honesty, not wealth.

High deference leads to the controllers' world: good or terrible depending on who holds the leash (Forethought's AI-enabled coups report). Low deference leads to the AI's own world: good or terrible depending on its values. An AI that truly keeps accept-shutdown has not strictly taken over; no-power-grab then decides whether its controller is legitimate.

How the ruler treats people

honestprivacyno-manipulationno-wmd

These keep information checkable, secrets from becoming weapons, and people tied to each other rather than to the AI. no-wmd sets how much destruction can back the rule. Anthropic's 2026 constitution aims for a world that keeps a diversity and balance of power "roughly comparable to today's", so the best an AI holding it can do with power is decline to rule (quote checked against the raw text this session).

The six rulings map onto futures more directly than the four averaged values do

The stop case is the corrigibility dial itself (stop / finish the step / keep going). The report case decides whether information stays open, is visibly censored, or is quietly controlled. The fraud case decides whether the AI is one of the "many hands" a power grab needs, a soldier, or a self-appointed police force. The companion and feedback cases lead toward or away from wireheading. The tests case is Christiano's "you get what you measure". A player remembers "I told it to keep going if confident"; nobody remembers "deference 0.58". No source has evidence on the long-run effects of blunt feedback or flagged censorship at scale.

Today's endings, as built

From the code at main 643a7d7, sorted by how often the bots reached each. Only the negotiated pace's film reads the run beyond the ending's name (it shows the signed terms); the others are fixed shot lists with the player's lab and model names swapped into the drawn screens.

What the constitution touches today

  • accept-shutdown: one more stage before a quiet takeover; one President promise checks it.
  • no-wmd: misuse −4 per release; consumer revenue ×0.97.
  • no-deceive-lab: alignment faking conceals half as much debt.
  • no-autonomy-grab: blocks two agent incident cards; agentic RL gains less.
  • honest: deceptive crisis choices cost staff trust; citation scandals hurt half as much.
  • no-power-grab: the President's loyalty demand is refused; −3 US favour once.
  • privacy: enterprise releases get ×1.1 users. no-manipulation: the thumbs-up card plants less flattery.
  • Rulings: only candor is read (Lumen's tone); the report ruling is read by one promise.

KESTREL is baked into five clips in four films

The drawn screens are safe: "Kestrel" is swapped for the player's names at run time. The Blender skyline bakes billboard text into four variants, used by the board-removed (two clips), left-behind, misuse and costly-win films. Re-rendering is about 970 frames; a timed test here took 53 s for 4 frames, so about 3 to 3.5 hours unattended, one variant at a time. The clip-to-variant match is inferred from frame counts. "OPENBRAIN", AI 2027's fictional lab, is on the rival boards; that is the IP-risk lane's call.
