# Game Dev Tycoon and Game Dev Story: how they present events, dilemmas, and notifications

Note on method: most sources below were retrieved with a WebFetch-style tool, which fetches a page and has a small model summarize it against a prompt — it does not hand back the page's raw text. That is a structural limit of the tool, not a choice to skim, but per the ⚠️ rule it means I did not see the literal wiki/article prose for those pages, only a processed account of it. I flag every such finding. Two fandom pages (`Random_Events`, `Messages`) blocked direct fetches (HTTP 402 and 403); `Random_Events` was recovered through a read-through proxy (`r.jina.ai`), which is marked separately below. Findings that come only from a WebSearch synthesis (no single page opened) are flagged as search-engine aggregation, which is weaker than an opened source and occasionally showed signs of the search tool inferring past its evidence (noted where it happened).

## Game Dev Tycoon: what kinds of events exist, and how is each presented?

### Takeaway
GDT's presentation layer is a single underlying `Notification` object (confirmed from the official modding API) that can either force itself onto the screen ("AutoPopup") or sit in a sidebar/message list depending on its `type` and the player's "Messages" settings; the visible variety players call "random events," "fan mail," "industry news," "G3," "contract offers," and "staff requests" are most likely different configurations of this one object rather than separate systems, though I could not fully confirm every category maps this way.

### Cited Findings
- ⚠️ (WebFetch-processed, and the source page itself is only reachable through a read-proxy after a direct 402 error) The Game Dev Tycoon Fandom wiki's Random Events page lists named events including: Improve Office Security (a staff request for a 50K security upgrade, quoted in-game line: "I have a knack for security and I think we could really do with some security upgrades..."), Women in Technology (a 100K sponsorship affecting a Good/Evil alignment stat), Nigerian Scam (a phishing request for 120K, written with deliberate grammar errors and misspelled company names), Patch Required (a mandatory bug-fix event costing 10K plus 5–20% of a game's original development cost when a released game has undiscovered bugs), Industrial Life Topic (buying stolen research for 5K–65K, moving the alignment stat), Industrial Sabotage (a competitor sabotage event costing 50K–170K), Air Conditioner (a multi-stage complaint that escalates from 90K to 180K if declined), Solar Panels from EPA (a sponsorship that reduces installation cost to 200K), Illegal Fan Game and Piracy Event (offering a choice to sue or warn over unauthorized use of company material/illegal copies), Technology Upgrade (150K office equipment unlocking office level 3), Technology Park (an 8M relocation available after year 13), Internal Coding Contest (a 90K prize donated to charity), and Legal Department (a patent-infringement claim with a pay-or-go-to-court choice). — [Random Events, Game Dev Tycoon Wiki (Fandom), via r.jina.ai proxy](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Random_Events)
- ⚠️ (same source/limits as above) The same page states random events begin after the company reaches the "Small Office" stage (office level 2) or later, matching the office-progression note already on file for this project (`office-progression-2026-09-25.md`), but the fetched summary explicitly did **not** contain the mechanics I most need — it does not say whether the game pauses, exactly how many choices are offered per event (my own prior local research on secondary screens describes GDT random events generically as "Dialogue and a choice with cash, fan or good/evil consequences," implying at least 2 options, but no source gives an exact count per event), or how outcomes are reported after the player picks. — [Random Events, Game Dev Tycoon Wiki (Fandom), via r.jina.ai proxy](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Random_Events)
- ⚠️ (WebFetch-processed) The official Greenheart Games modding API's `Notification` object (this is the real underlying data/UI class in the shipped game, exposed for modders) has these fields: `header` (title), `text` (body, supports a `{n}` token to split content across multiple screens/pages within one notification), `buttonText` (defaults to "OK"), `weeksUntilFired` (a delay before the notification fires, default 0), `image` (a 200×200 px image shown on the left side of the popup), `previewImage` (a 130×130 px thumbnail used when the notification sits in a sidebar rather than popping up), `type` (a category — named types include Default, PlatformNews, IndustryNews, Events, and AutoPopup), and `options` (2–3 choice buttons) with a required `sourceId` when options are used so the game can route the player's choice back to a handler. Actions available after a choice include `adjustCash()`, `adjustHype()`, `adjustFans()`, and `setFlag()`. — [Notification, greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI Wiki (GitHub)](https://github.com/greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI/wiki/Notification)
- ⚠️ (WebFetch-processed) The same page states directly: "Setting the type of a notification will affect whether the notification will auto-open or appear in the sidebar according to the players 'Messages' settings," and that the `AutoPopup` type "forces immediate display without player override." This is the clearest evidence found that GDT has (a) a sidebar/list where non-urgent notifications wait, (b) a player-controlled "Messages" setting governing whether things pop up or queue, and (c) a small number of notifications (AutoPopup ones — most likely the story-relevant ones like piracy, lawsuits, bankruptcy) that always interrupt regardless of that setting. — [Notification, greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI Wiki (GitHub)](https://github.com/greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI/wiki/Notification)
- The dedicated `GDT.addEvent` wiki page does not exist — fetching it returned GitHub's "Create new page" form, meaning no one has written that page; the general "Advanced Modding" page only confirms "a bunch of events defined on the GDT object" exist for modders to hook via `GDT.on`, without listing categories or a schema for defining new ones. — [Advanced Modding, greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI Wiki (GitHub)](https://github.com/greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI/wiki/Advanced-Modding); [GDT.addEvent (unwritten page), greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI Wiki (GitHub)](https://github.com/greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI/wiki/GDT.addEvent)
- A WebSearch-only synthesis (no single page opened, so weaker sourcing) reports the `GDT.eventKeys.gameplay` namespace includes hooks such as `afterGameReview`, `beforeGameReview`, `afterReleaseGame`, `beforeReleaseGame`, `contractFinished`, `contractStarted`, `engineFinished`, `engineStarted`, `featureFinished`, `gameDefinitionCanceled`, `staffApplicantsGenerated`, `staffHired`, and `staffFired`. This confirms contracts and staff hiring fire discrete named events in the engine, consistent with "contract offers" and "staff requests" being real, separate notification triggers, but I have not seen the raw page listing these to check completeness. — [GDT, greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI Wiki (GitHub), via WebSearch synthesis](https://github.com/greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI/wiki/GDT)
- ⚠️ (WebSearch synthesis) G3, the annual trade-show event, presents "a window detailing the booth size they would like to purchase for G3: Small, Medium, or Large" at a fixed point in the calendar (Month 5, Week 1 per this source, with the show itself in Month 6, Week 2); this matches this project's own prior research file, which already logged G3 as "Automatic popup once a year... gated by fans." Running one's own convention (an R&D-lab unlock) reuses "the same booth popup." — [G3, Game Dev Tycoon Wiki (Fandom), via WebSearch synthesis](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/G3); consistent with [`secondary-screens-2026-09-25.md`, this project's local notes]
- Steam players describe the popups as unwanted interruptions rather than praised content: one thread's original poster states plainly, "It really breaks game immersion and they are really very ennoying [sic]! So many useless clicks and wasted time," specifically citing the typing-out text animation as part of the annoyance; a reply's workaround is to set a 42-year game length at the start, which reportedly reduces (but does not remove) popup frequency, since some events appear to be scheduled relative to total game length rather than purely random. There is no in-game option to disable popups, per this thread. — [Please stop popup messages, Steam Community discussion for Game Dev Tycoon, via WebFetch](https://steamcommunity.com/app/239820/discussions/0/864978110185513334/)
- A GameCritics.com review (a harsh 3/10, already logged as a strong critical source in this project's prior research) states directly about the piracy/DRM/lawsuit-style controversy events: "Every time I was faced with a controversy and thought things were about to get interesting, the issues were swiftly swept under the rug." — [Game Dev Tycoon Review, GameCritics.com, via WebFetch](https://gamecritics.com/tayo-stalnaker/game-dev-tycoon-review/)
- ⚠️ (WebSearch synthesis, not tied to one opened page) Other review summaries describe piracy-related pop-ups as offering choices such as adopting DRM software or threatening the modding community with lawsuits, and characterize these choices as having little measurable effect on sales and no further narrative follow-up. I could not independently confirm this against a single fully-opened review, so it is included as a secondhand/aggregated claim rather than a directly cited quote.
- A hint/tip system exists that shows textual quality signals during development (`+++`/`++`/`--`-style ratings) and genre/platform compatibility notes under console choices; it is off by default for some players and can be toggled or restored via a community-made "hints mod," per forum discussion. This is a distinct, lower-stakes presentation channel from the costed-choice event popups — it reads as inline advisory text near the relevant UI control, not a modal dialog with consequences. — [How to enable hints? (+++, -- etc.), Greenheart Games Forum, via WebSearch synthesis](https://forum.greenheartgames.com/t/how-to-enable-hints-etc/13508); [General Info & Hints, Game Dev Tycoon Wiki (Fandom), via WebSearch synthesis](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/General_Info_%26_Hints)

### Inferences
- The `type` field on `Notification` (Default / PlatformNews / IndustryNews / Events / AutoPopup) most likely corresponds exactly to the player-facing categories the community calls "industry news," "platform announcements," and "random events" — i.e., these are not separate UI systems but the same popup/sidebar component configured differently. This is my inference from the modAPI documentation; no source states this mapping outright, so it should be treated as a plausible reconstruction, not a confirmed one.
- Given `options` requires only 2–3 choices and a `sourceId` to route the response, and every named random event I found (security upgrade, Nigerian scam, sabotage, patent claim, etc.) reduces to "pay / decline" or "pay / decline / a third qualified option," GDT's event choices are consistently binary-or-ternary, matching the "2-3 costly choices" shape the target game (Game Night: AI Lab) is already designed around.
- The existence of a "Messages settings" the player can adjust (per the Notification page) implies GDT deliberately gives players a way to demote noisy notification types out of the interrupting popup path and into a passive sidebar list — i.e., the queueing/interruption balance is a designed, player-tunable knob, not a fixed behavior. This directly informs the "does it queue or interrupt" question below.

### Gaps
- No source (opened, proxied, or searched) states outright whether GDT's game-time simulation pauses while an event/notification dialog is open. One community mod's own description says its "pause" state "overrides any other pause in the game ... no matter what dialogs are opened or closed," which implies dialogs already have some baseline pause behavior for the mod to override — but this is one line from a mod's README, not a direct statement about default behavior, so I list it only as a weak signal, not a finding. — [dgalaktionov/TimeMod, GitHub, via WebSearch snippet](https://github.com/dgalaktionov/TimeMod)
- No source gives an exact, universal choice-count (e.g., "always exactly 2" or "always exactly 3") across every named event; the evidence supports "2 or 3" but not a single fixed number.
- Whether the player can defer (postpone, "remind me later") an AutoPopup-type event, versus only choosing among the given options immediately, was not found in any source.
- Fan mail specifically (mentioned in the task's key questions) was not documented by name in any source I reached; it may be folded into the general Notification/sidebar system, or it may not exist as a named GDT feature at all — I cannot tell which.

## In GDT, how are notifications queued — is there a stack/queue, can the player read them later, is there a log?

### Takeaway
Yes — the modding API confirms a sidebar-based queue exists for non-urgent notification types, governed by a player-configurable "Messages" setting, though I could not confirm whether this sidebar is a persistent, scrollable log of history or only holds currently-pending items.

### Cited Findings
- ⚠️ (WebFetch-processed) As already quoted above, the `Notification` type field determines "whether the notification will auto-open or appear in the sidebar according to the players 'Messages' settings," and a `previewImage` (130×130 px) is specifically described as being "for sidebar thumbnail" use — i.e., a visually distinct, smaller presentation exists for the queued/sidebar state versus the full popup state. — [Notification, greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI Wiki (GitHub)](https://github.com/greenheartgames/gdt-modAPI/wiki/Notification)
- A direct fetch of the Fandom wiki's `Messages` page returned an HTTP 403 (blocked) even through the read-proxy, so I could not confirm from the wiki itself whether this sidebar is a persistent inbox/log the player can revisit at any time, whether items expire, or how many can stack at once. — [Messages, Game Dev Tycoon Wiki (Fandom) — page blocked, not read](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Messages)

### Inferences
- Because `weeksUntilFired` exists as a field (a delay before a notification fires), and because a sidebar mode exists at all, GDT's notification system is built to hold multiple pending items rather than only ever showing one thing at a time — this supports a "queue," but I have no confirmation of stacking behavior (e.g., what happens if two AutoPopup events would fire in the same week).

### Gaps
- Whether there is a true historical log (read something after it has already been actioned) versus only a queue of not-yet-seen items is unconfirmed.
- Whether AutoPopup events can stack/queue behind each other if two fire close together, or whether the second is dropped/delayed, is unconfirmed.

## Game Dev Story: how does it present events, and how does it keep them inside the office scene?

### Takeaway
Verified detail on Game Dev Story's actual on-screen presentation is thin across the sources I could reach — wiki pages document *what* triggers (Gamedex convention, Global Game Awards, a recurring vendor, magazine reviews) and their numeric effects, but explicitly do not describe the visual/UI form those take, and the one page most likely to answer this (Magazine Reviews) is a stub. TouchArcade's original review independently confirms the game is a small, static isometric/pixel-art office view rather than one that cuts away to other scenes, which is suggestive but not a direct statement about event presentation.

### Cited Findings
- ⚠️ (WebFetch-processed) The Kairosoft wiki records that a "Gamedex" convention runs annually starting the first week of July, letting the company gain popularity through fan bases or a specific game gain hype; the "Global Game Awards" run annually in December, and a company only enters once a game has reached the Hall of Fame (a review-score threshold), with a higher score threshold (36+) needed to actually win Game of the Year; a "secretary" character is described as giving in-game advice (example line: "There's no point in advertising until you've released a game"); and a vendor selling "Pumpkin Products" appears annually in the first week of May. None of this confirms the visual form (popup vs. in-scene) — the summary explicitly states presentation details were not in the source content. — [Game Dev Story, The Kairosoft Wiki, via WebFetch](https://kairosoft.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Dev_Story)
- ⚠️ (WebFetch-processed) The dedicated Magazine Reviews page on the Kairosoft Fandom wiki is marked as a stub and contains only a table of review text/point values; it explicitly does not describe whether a review is shown as a popup, a text box, or a visiting character, nor whether gameplay pauses, nor who "delivers" the review. This is a confirmed **gap in the source itself**, not just in my reading of it. — [Magazine Reviews (Game Dev Story), Kairosoft Wiki (Fandom), via WebFetch](https://kairosoft.fandom.com/wiki/Magazine_Reviews_(Game_Dev_Story))
- TouchArcade's original 2010 review states the game's "16-bit style pixel graphics ... are a far cry of what is capable from even the oldest iOS devices," and separately notes "the screen size is also oddly small, with plenty of wasted space on the top and bottom" — this is a review of the base visual presentation (a small, static isometric office view), not of event dialogs specifically, but it corroborates that the game's visual "world" is a single small office scene throughout, which is consistent with the premise that events read as speech bubbles/overlays inside that one scene rather than full-screen cutaways (this last clause is my inference, not the review's claim). — [‘Game Dev Story’ Review, TouchArcade, via WebFetch](https://toucharcade.com/2010/10/12/game-dev-story-review-create-your-own-game-company)
- ⚠️ (WebSearch synthesis, not independently opened) The game is generally described as using speech bubbles above staff sprites to show at-a-glance activity/mood, and an isometric top-down view of the office is the base display throughout — consistent with the office-based presentation this project is already modeling its own game on, but I was not able to open a source that describes this mechanic in enough detail to quote directly.

### Inferences
- Given that Kairosoft's own review-score and convention mechanics are richly documented on wikis but their UI presentation is not, and given TouchArcade's confirmation of a small, fixed, low-resolution office view as the whole game screen, my working inference is that Game Dev Story deliberately keeps almost everything (reviews, awards, vendor visits) as small overlays, text boxes, or animated sprite behavior layered on top of the same static office view, rather than full-screen modal interruptions — this would explain why no source needed to describe a separate "event screen." This is an inference from the shape of the evidence (rich mechanical documentation + total silence on presentation, in a game whose entire screen is one small scene), not a directly cited fact, and it should be treated with real caution given how thin the direct evidence is.

### Gaps
- No source documents whether Game Dev Story time pauses during any event, dialog, or review reveal.
- No source documents the exact number of choices (if any) in Game Dev Story dilemma-style events, or whether costs/consequences are shown before the player picks — the task's premise that GDS has "dilemma" events with visible choices was not confirmed by any source I reached; the events I found (Gamedex, Global Game Awards, magazine reviews, the Pumpkin vendor) read as automatic/scored outcomes rather than player choices with options.
- "Boost" moments were not documented in any source I reached under that name.
- The office-visitor mechanic and "Record Visitors" were named once by a WebSearch summary but never confirmed by an opened page — genuine gap.

## Both games: which presentation conventions do reviewers/players praise or criticize?

### Takeaway
Every piece of direct critical commentary I could source is about Game Dev Tycoon, and it is consistently negative about the *popup* form specifically — players call it immersion-breaking and click-heavy, and a professional review calls the controversy-style events narratively hollow. I found no comparable direct reviewer commentary about Game Dev Story's event presentation (as opposed to its overall repetitiveness or dated visuals), which is itself worth reporting as an absence.

### Cited Findings
- (repeated from above, consolidated here) Steam thread original poster: popups "really break game immersion" and are "really very ennoying [sic]... So many useless clicks and wasted time," with the typing-text animation named as a specific irritant; no in-game toggle to disable them exists per that thread. — [Please stop popup messages, Steam Community, via WebFetch](https://steamcommunity.com/app/239820/discussions/0/864978110185513334/)
- GameCritics.com (3/10 review): controversy-style events (piracy, DRM, lawsuits) are "swiftly swept under the rug" just as they seem to be getting interesting — a direct criticism that the choices lack meaningful follow-through. — [Game Dev Tycoon Review, GameCritics.com](https://gamecritics.com/tayo-stalnaker/game-dev-tycoon-review/)
- Already logged in this project's own prior research (`secondary-screens-2026-09-25.md`, itself citing the same GameCritics review plus Steam threads): random events are "the most-cited source of surprise, even by a harsh 3/10 review," and are named as one of the few things that keeps the mid-game from feeling solved — i.e., the criticism is specifically about *depth/follow-through* and *interruption friction*, not that events exist at all; their existence is credited as good pacing design even by critical reviewers.
- ⚠️ (WebSearch synthesis) TouchArcade's 2017 review of *Game Dev Tycoon* (comparing it explicitly to *Game Dev Story*) exists per search results, but I did not open it directly, so I cannot cite specific presentation commentary from it beyond its title implying GDT is being framed as the spiritual successor players of GDS would want. — [‘Game Dev Tycoon’ Review – If You Liked ‘Game Dev Story’ You’ll Love ‘Game Dev Tycoon’, TouchArcade — title/existence only, not opened](https://toucharcade.com/2017/11/28/game-dev-tycoon-review-if-you-liked-game-dev-story-youll-love-game-dev-tycoon/)

### Inferences
- The complaint pattern across both the Steam thread and the professional review converges on the same root cause: GDT's popups interrupt the player (breaking flow/immersion) but the payoff for engaging with them is perceived as shallow (no lasting narrative consequence, and per the earlier aggregated claim, limited measurable mechanical effect). For the target game's design, this suggests the risk to avoid is not "having interrupting cards" per se — critics credit them as good pacing — but making the cost of engaging with a card feel disconnected from a visible, lasting outcome.

### Gaps
- No player or critic commentary specifically about Game Dev Story's event/notification presentation (as distinct from general repetitiveness or dated graphics) was found in the sources I could reach.
- I could not confirm whether GDT's popup complaints are about frequency (too many events), duration (the typing-text animation cited above), or genuine lack of choice-meaningfulness, beyond the specific quotes already given — a fuller review sweep (e.g., opening more Steam Community reviews or the Metacritic user-review page directly) would sharpen this but was outside the ~15-tool-call budget for this pass.
