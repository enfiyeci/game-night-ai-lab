# Game Dev Tycoon: how the office and the player's options grow (2026-09-25)

Why: the owner said the first pass of the era-by-era office was "a lil too minimal" and asked what
new fields open up as Game Dev Tycoon (GDT) progresses. This is the research behind the next pass.

Provenance: gathered by a research subagent (Claude Sonnet) that read each page below in full in
the built-in browser. It is the subagent's synthesis, not re-verified line by line by the
orchestrator. The gating numbers agree across four independent sources (the wiki's Unlocks table,
its HINTS_GUIDE page, a Steam guide and a GameFAQs walkthrough).

## Office stages

| Stage | Unlock | Staff cap | What visibly changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Garage | Start | 1 (the player only) | A tiny space: one computer, a workbench, a parked DeLorean half under a cloth ([Garage](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Garage)) |
| 2 First office | $1M cash; the move costs $150K | 5 (after a free Staff Management training) | A real office; hiring, Medium games and publishing deals unlock ([Unlocks](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Unlocks), [Training](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Training)) |
| 3 "PC upgrade" | Office 2, 2 staff, $5M, year 11 month 6 or later; costs $150K | 5 | Same floor plan; the wiki says "the building doesn't actually change", but the wall posters swap (Wing Commander and Doom → Half-Life and Theme Hospital) ([Unlocks](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Unlocks), [Easter Eggs](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Easter_Eggs)) |
| 4 New building | Office 3, 4 staff, $16M, year 13 month 9 or later; costs $8M | 7 | A new building in a "technology park" ([R&D Lab](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/R%26D_Lab), [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=325266457)) |
| R&D lab (room in office 4) | A Design Specialist | — | A new room with its own research tree: MMO, AAA games, own convention, engine licensing ([R&D Lab](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/R%26D_Lab)) |
| Hardware lab (room in office 4) | R&D lab + a Technology Specialist + "Custom Hardware" research | — | A new room that builds your own console ([Hardware Lab](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Hardware_Lab)) |

## Menu-only unlocks (no new geometry)

Target audience (year 3), casual games (year 3), marketing (year 4), sequels (year 8), multi-genre
(year 12), multi-platform (year 17), Large games (4+ staff), AAA games (a Large game scoring five
10s), engine features (by use or R&D research), training tiers (stat caps 500 → 700 → 900, one
tier per office move), the yearly G3 expo, and later hosting your own convention (R&D research).
Sources: [Unlocks](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Unlocks), [AAA](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/AAA),
[G3](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/G3), [Training](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Training),
[General Info & Hints](https://gamedevtycoon.fandom.com/wiki/General_Info_%26_Hints).

## Design moves that transfer

1. Moves are gated by cash, headcount and date together, so a windfall cannot skip the pacing.
2. The room caps the staff: "we need a bigger office" is a real bottleneck.
3. Late fields arrive as rooms that need both the space and a qualified specialist.
4. Not every stage needs new geometry: a renovation plus a poster swap still reads as progress.
5. Era-dated wall art makes "what year is it" legible at a glance.
6. Industry events mature: attend the expo early, host your own convention late.
