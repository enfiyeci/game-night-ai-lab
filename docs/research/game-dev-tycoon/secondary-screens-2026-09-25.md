# Game Dev Tycoon: the screens besides the office (2026-09-25)

Why: the owner remembered GDT having "other sections to look up or other financial tracking page", and worried the game "might get a lil boring if they only see this" (the office view). This note records which secondary screens GDT actually has, so the choice of extra screens for our game rests on the real reference.

Provenance: a Sonnet research subagent's sweep, not re-read line by line by the orchestrator. The orchestrator spot-checked one source directly (the Steam thread "Actual Competition/Other Companies?"), which agrees: players say you are "the only game developer in the entire world" and competition shows only as an unexplained "currently most popular game" rank. Treat the rest as the subagent's claims until traced.

## Headline

GDT has **no finance or cash-history chart, no statistics screen, no yearly "top games" chart and no competitor or market-share view**. Those belong to Kairosoft's Game Dev Story, and GDT players name their absence as a reason the mid-game gets repetitive. The nearest thing to "financial tracking" is the **Game History** tab: a table of every released game with its sales, cost, profit, score, fans and top sales rank.

## What players say keeps the mid-game alive, and what doesn't

- Discovery: companies start knowing five topics; unlocking the rest and the game-report insights are the "still learning" hook.
- Random events are the most-cited source of surprise, even by a harsh 3/10 review (gamecritics.com).
- Milestones (office moves, labs, new research) are spread over the whole run.
- Counter-evidence: once the scoring formula is solved, GDT gets repetitive, and players invent their own restrictions to add challenge. Several sources tie this to GDT lacking the extra views (finance charts, competitor rankings).
