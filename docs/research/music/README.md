# Background music research (2026-09-26)

The owner asked for background music "inspired by Game Dev Tycoon" that the game may ship for
free (GitHub Pages, itch.io). One Sonnet research pass wrote the findings below. They are
**unverified raw output**: the orchestrating session did not re-open the sources. Claims resting
on a partial read carry ⚠️, and the agent's coverage statement is at the end. No track has been
chosen or downloaded yet.

## Game Dev Tycoon's own music

- Greenheart's forum post "The Music and Art of Game Dev Tycoon" says the background music was
  bought from the melodyloops.com stock library, with sound effects by Joachim Lipnegg or from
  free sources. The Video Game Audio Wiki credits Alex Lisenkov and Jack White for the music.
- Alexander Lisenkov's melodyloops and Magnatune pages describe lounge and dance electronic music
  with elements of jazz; his catalogue includes business cues such as "Getting To Success",
  "Workflow" and "Startup".
- Reviews agree it is gentle synth or lounge music that wears thin over long sessions: GameGrin
  calls it "laid back elevator style music", SomeAwesome says the tracks are soothing for about 20
  minutes before the loop grates, and Switch Player also complains about the "repetitive blips" of
  the bubble sound effects.
- No official soundtrack was released (Greenheart forum thread 4530).
- ⚠️ No source found says whether the music changes by office or stage, ducks under dialogs, or
  plays stingers on releases. A single looping playlist is an inference from reviews, not a fact.

Takeaway for us: several tracks in rotation at a low volume, a mute control, and different moods
for later eras would avoid the one complaint every review makes.

## Suggested directions

- **A, closest to how Game Dev Tycoon sounds:** Wallpaper as the main loop, Local Forecast –
  Elevator in rotation.
- **B, closest to Game Dev Tycoon's actual composer (lounge jazz):** Bossa Antigua with Corporate
  Lounge as an electronic alternate.
- **C, later and darker eras:** Corporate Ladder plus one or two electronic loops from the CC0
  collections.
