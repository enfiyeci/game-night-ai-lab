# Era 1 raw research: business, legal, policy, governance and people (December 2022 – December 2023)

Raw output of a research subagent (Sonnet), 2026-09-26, lightly condensed by the orchestrator.
**Unverified by the orchestrator**; cite the distilled candidate list, not this file. ⚠️ marks are the
subagent's own. Quarter mapping used by the game: Q1 = Dec 2022–Feb 2023, Q2 = Mar–May 2023,
Q3 = Jun–Aug 2023, Q4 = Sep–Dec 2023. Ranked by dilemma quality.

1. **OpenAI board fires, then rehires, Sam Altman (Q4; November 17–22, 2023).** The board said he
   "was not consistently candid"; over 700 of about 770 staff signed a letter threatening to follow
   him to Microsoft, which offered them jobs; Sutskever signed and apologised; Altman returned with a
   new board. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Removal_of_Sam_Altman_from_OpenAI (read in full).
2. **Microsoft's multibillion investment in OpenAI (Q1; January 23, 2023).** Azure became the
   exclusive cloud; about $10B as reported by the press (the post itself says only "multiyear,
   multibillion dollar"). Dilemma: capital and one-cloud lock-in versus slower independence. Source:
   https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2023/01/23/microsoftandopenaiextendpartnership/ (read in full).
3. **Altman's Senate testimony (Q2; May 16, 2023).** Asked for licensing and an agency that can
   revoke licences; about 60 lawmakers briefed beforehand; a cordial hearing. Dilemma: ask for rules
   you can shape (critics call it a moat) or resist and look reckless. Source:
   https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-ceo-embraces-government-regulation-senate-hearing-rcna83931 (read in full).
4. **FLI six-month pause letter (Q2; March 22, 2023).** Over 30,000 signatures; no lab paused.
   Source: https://futureoflife.org/open-letter/pause-giant-ai-experiments/ (read in full).
5. **Executive Order 14110 (Q4; October 30, 2023).** Defense Production Act reporting of large
   training runs, weight ownership and red-team results to Commerce; rescinded January 2025.
   Sources: https://www.dwt.com/blogs/artificial-intelligence-law-advisor/2023/11/executive-order-safe-artificial-intelligence
   (read in full); Wikipedia EO 14110 page (read; thin on specifics). ⚠️ The Federal Register text
   was not read (bot check).
6. **New York Times v. OpenAI and Microsoft (Q4; December 27, 2023).** Filed after licensing talks
   broke down; "billions of dollars" sought; near-verbatim reproduction alleged. Source:
   https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/new-york-sues-microsoft-chatgpt-maker-openai-copyright-infringement-rcna131289 (read in full).
7. **Writers' strike settles with AI terms (Q3–Q4; May – September 24, 2023).** AI can't be a
   credited writer; disclosure required. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/26/writers-strike-over-ai/ (read in full).
8. **SAG-AFTRA strike over digital replicas (Q3–Q4; July 14 – November 9, 2023).** Consent per use
   of a replica. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_SAG-AFTRA_strike (read in full; ⚠️ final
   contract clauses thinly covered).
9. **Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy (Q4; September 19, 2023).** Self-binding safety levels;
   won't deploy an ASL-3 model with meaningful catastrophic misuse risk. Source:
   https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropics-responsible-scaling-policy (read in full).
10. **Getty Images v. Stability AI (Q1; January 2023).** Over 12 million photos allegedly copied
    after Stability declined a licence. Source:
    https://voicebot.ai/2023/01/18/getty-images-sues-stability-ai-for-generative-ai-arts-alleged-copyright-violations/ (read in full).
11. **Andersen v. Stability AI, Midjourney, DeviantArt (Q1; January 13, 2023).** First artists'
    class action. ⚠️ Details from a search summary; the DWT page returned HTTP 451.
12. **Authors Guild v. OpenAI (Q4; September 20, 2023).** Grisham, Martin, Picoult and others;
    shadow-library books alleged. Source: https://authorsguild.org/news/ag-and-authors-file-class-action-suit-against-openai/ (read in full).
13. **Silverman, Golden, Kadrey v. OpenAI and Meta (Q3; July 7, 2023).** Pirated books alleged;
    neither company disclosed its training data. Source:
    https://www.semafor.com/article/07/10/2023/sarah-silverman-suing-openai-meta (read in full).
14. **FTC civil investigative demand to OpenAI (Q3; leaked July 13, 2023).** Asked about data
    sourcing, privacy, the March 2023 bug that exposed chat histories (the subagent wrote "March
    2020"; corrected here), hallucination complaints and accuracy claims. Source:
    https://www.afslaw.com/perspectives/privacy-counsel/leaked-ftc-civil-investigative-demand-openai-provides-rare-preliminary (read in full).
15. **US chip export controls tightened (Q4; October 17, 2023).** Closed the interconnect loophole
    that China-specific chips used; 43 more countries covered. Dilemma for a lab: stockpile before
    supply tightens or wait. Source: https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/bis-2023-update-explainer/ (read in full).
16. **Bletchley Park summit (Q4; November 1–2, 2023).** 28 countries plus the EU; labs agreed to
    pre-release government testing. Source: gov.uk Bletchley Declaration page (read in full).
17. **CAIS extinction-risk statement (Q2; May 30, 2023).** Signed by the CEOs of OpenAI, Google
    DeepMind and Anthropic. Source: https://aistatement.com/ (read in full).
18. **Hinton leaves Google to speak freely (Q2; May 1, 2023).** Source:
    https://www.technologyreview.com/2023/05/01/1072478/deep-learning-pioneer-geoffrey-hinton-quits-google/ (read in full).
19. **White House voluntary commitments (Q3; July 21, 2023).** Seven companies; outside red-teaming,
    weight protection, watermarking; no enforcement. Source: presidency.ucsb.edu fact sheet (read in full).
20. **Frontier Model Forum (Q3; July 26, 2023).** Source: blogs.microsoft.com on-the-issues post (read in full).
21. **Amazon invests up to $4B in Anthropic (Q4; September 25, 2023).** AWS primary cloud and
    Trainium chips. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/25/amazon-to-invest-up-to-4-billion-in-ai-startup-anthropic (read in full).
22. **Google commits up to $2B to Anthropic (Q4; October 27, 2023).** A backer that is also a
    rival. Source: pymnts.com (read in full).
23. **EU AI Act political deal (Q4; December 8–9, 2023).** France, Germany and Italy, lobbied by
    home labs such as Mistral, pushed for lighter foundation-model rules. Source: infosecurity-magazine.com (read in full).
24. **Reddit charges for its API to stop free AI scraping (Q2–Q3; April – July 2023; blackout June
    12).** Source: https://www.theregister.com/2023/06/05/reddit_blackout_planned_over_appkilling/ (read in full).
25. **Stack Overflow moderator strike over AI answers (Q3; June 5 – August 2, 2023).** Source: oecd.ai incident page (read in full).
